ABOUT THE PROJECT

Ideas for better thinking

Frases do Olavo is an independent editorial project that collects quotations attributed to Olavo de Carvalho and publishes analysis, reporting and commentary. Quotations and articles are distinct: editorial pieces may cover current issues even when they are not directly connected to a quotation by the author.

Bruno Stefano is a Software Engineering student, a professional poker dealer, and an inexperienced diesel-engine mechanic.

What you will find here

The site combines a searchable quotation collection with articles on politics and institutions, technology and digital security, economics, culture, society and sports. The aim is to provide context, explain arguments and encourage critical reading while distinguishing documented facts, interpretations and opinions.

Editorial standards

Quotations attributed to an author should be checked against reliable sources whenever possible. Articles about public events should distinguish facts, interpretation and opinion and cite verifiable sources for significant factual claims. Some attributions may require confirmation, so readers should consult the original source before reusing a quotation.

Independence

This is an independent editorial project. Publishing a quotation or article does not automatically mean endorsing every opinion attributed to the cited author, nor does it represent the author, their family or any institution officially.

Corrections and suggestions

To report an error, question an attribution or suggest a topic, visit Contact. Corrections are reviewed before editorial changes are made.
